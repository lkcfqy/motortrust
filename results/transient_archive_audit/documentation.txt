Data in .mat files:
ialbt_meas	...	Alpha and beta axis currents (respectively) transformed from the measured stator currents
idq_meas	...	Direct and quadrature axis currents (respectively) transformed from the measured stator currents
if_meas		...	Measured fault current
SinCos		...	Angle tracking observer outputs: sine and cosine (respectively) of the electrical angle
ualbt		...	Alpha and beta axis voltages (respectively) generated by the control algorithm
udq		...	Direct and quadrature axis voltages (respectively) generated by the control algorithm
we		...	Angle tracking observer outputs: estimated electrical angular velocity
