Journal of Magnetics

  • The Journal of Magnetics, published by the Korean Magnetics Society, covers all research areas related to magnetism. Since 1996, the Journal of Magnetics has been published quarterly, and the official language is English.
  • The scope of the Journal of Magnetics is all magnetism related research, including magnetic theories, materials, and properties. It also covers magnetic recording materials, technologies, spin electronics, measurements and applications.
  • The Journal of Magnetics is registered on SCOPUS and SCIE.
