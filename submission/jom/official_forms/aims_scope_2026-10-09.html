Aims and scope

The published articles in the Journal of Magnetics are academic research papers in English, and their topics include the magnetic theory, magnetic materials and their properties, magnetic recording materials and technology, spin electronics, measurements and applications, and other relevant topics.

The papers in the Journal of Magnetics are classified into three categories; academic research articles, academic review articles, and technical reviews.

Journal of Magnetics was founded in 1996 and has been published quarterly. (Journal of Magnetics, ISSN 1226-1750(Print)/ ISSN 2233-6656(Online)) Some, or all, of the articles in this journal are indexed in Science Citation Index-Expanded (SCIE) in the Web of Science, Scopus, ISI Alerting Service, Materials Science Citation Index (MSCI), DOI/Crossref, KCI, and Google Scholar.

The range of categories in JOM is extensive including

  • Magnetic Thin Films & Nanostructures
  • Multiferroic and Magnetic Oxides, Superconductivity
  • Spin Electronics & Application
  • Modeling And Computational Magnetism
  • Characterization & Imaging
  • Magnetic Recording
  • Permanent Magnetic Materials & Applications
  • Soft Magnetic Materials & Applications
  • Other Magnetic Materials & Non-Recording Applications
  • Sensor, High Frequency & Power Devices
  • Bio Magnetism & Applications
  • Magnetics in Medical Science
  • Interdisciplinary Topics etc.

한국자기학회지(Journal of the Korean Magnetics Society)는 국문 또는 영문으로 작성된 자기분야 논문들을 발표하는 한국자기학회의 대표적인 국문학술지로써, 자기이론, 자성재료 및 물성, 정보기록, 스핀, 응용 및 측정 등 관련 주제에 이르기까지 폭넓은 자기분야의 논문을 게재하고 있습니다.

한국자기학회지에 발표되는 논문은 연구논문(Research article), 해설논문(Review article), 기술해설(Technical review)로 구분됩니다.

한국자기학회지는 1991년 창간되어 현재까지 격월로 발간되고 있고(한국자기학회지, ISSN 1598-5385) 2002년부터 한국연구재단(KCI) 등재지로 유지되고 있습니다.
