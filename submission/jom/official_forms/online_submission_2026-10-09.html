Online Submission

Instructions on submitting manuscript

The submission of papers to the Journal of Magnetics is possible only for members who have joined the Society

The papers must be completed according to the paper guidelines of the Journal of Magnetics. Manuscript file in the form of a single file containing all of Title, Abstract and keywords, Address, Figures, Formulae and Tables.

  • Download Template File for doc

We accept .hwp file or MS word file. We do not accept the .docx file format created by using MS 2007/2008.

  1. Connect to Online Submission, then fill in all required fields and attach files.
  2. Please check the "Copyright Transfer Form" and "Author's Check List Form" in online submission system.
  3. In order to confirm your submission, please check your manuscript status after submission.
