Authors Responsibilities

Manuscript content and accuracy are the authors’ responsibility. Manuscripts must be submitted solely to the Journal of Magnetics(JOM). A manuscript is considered for publication with the understanding that it has not been published in its entirety or any portion thereof (this includes tables and figures) previously in print or electronic form and is not under consideration by another publication or electronic medium. Neither this manuscript nor a manuscript with similar content has been published or is being considered for publication in any language, except as an abstract or thesis. A paper should contain sufficient detail and references to public sources of information to permit others to repeat the work.

Statement and opinions given in work published by the Journal of Magnetics are the expressions of the authors. Responsibility for the contents of published papers rests upon the authors, not the Journal of Magnetics . The Peer review is essential to scientific and technical discourse. All data should be presented upon request by the editor, to facilitate the review process. The Financial support of the work being reported and of the authors should be clearly acknowledged, as should any potential conflict of interest. Authors have an obligation to correct errors promptly. Authors should not discuss any aspect of a manuscript under evaluation with reviewers of the submitted manuscript. If authors make use of charts, photographs, or other graphical or textual material from previously published material, the authors are responsible for obtaining written permission to use the material in the manuscript. Plagiarism is unacceptable.

한국자기학회에서 출판된 논문의 내용은 저자의 표현으로 출판된 논문의 내용에 대한 책임은 한국자기학회가 아닌 저자에게 있습니다. 동료평가는 과학 및 기술적 담론에 필수사항입니다.

모든 데이터는 검토과정을 위해 편집자의 요청에 따라 제시될 수 있어야 합니다. 연구비 출처와 관련 이해상충을 공개해야 합니다. 저자들은 오류를 즉시 수정할 의무가 있습니다. 저자는 제출된 원고를 심사자와 어떠한 측면에 관해서도 논의해서는 안됩니다. 저자가 이전에 출판되었던 자료의 차트, 그림 또는 기타 그래픽 또는 텍스트 자료를 사용하는 경우 해당 자료를 사용할 수 있는 서면 허가를 받을 책임이 있으며 표절은 용납할 수 없습니다.
