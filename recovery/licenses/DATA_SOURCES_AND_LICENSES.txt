INTEGRITY AND SCOPE

- Exact source-record metadata and license names checked for this notice are
  retained in SOURCE_METADATA.json.
- FETCH_MANIFEST.json records official license URLs, fetched byte counts,
  retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 values. License text files were saved without
  editing; the upstream LPTN MIT file was copied byte-for-byte.
- Existing MotorTrust manifests and metadata record the original data hashes.
- Folder names and generated table titles do not replace original creator credit.
- Preserve the source license and attribution of a dataset when copying its files
  into a combined research snapshot.
